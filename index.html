Meet Jessica

A calm, safe space to talk, heal, and find your way forward.

I am an ASCHP Specialist Wellness Counsellor, and I walk alongside people through life's challenges and difficult seasons. I believe everyone deserves a safe, supportive space where they can feel heard, understood and accepted.

I help people make sense of their experiences, reconnect with themselves, and find the threads of resilience, strength and hope already woven through their story. My approach is warm, authentic and client-centred: I meet you where you are and go at your pace.

The name is inspired by Kintsugi, the Japanese art of mending broken pottery with gold. The cracks are not hidden; they become part of something stronger. Healing does not erase what happened. It helps you carry it differently.

  • BA Psychology & Sociology (NWU), Cum Laude
  • BA Honours in Counselling Psychology (UNISA), Cum Laude
  • STREPCO Course, Helios Training Centre
  • Registered with ASCHP as a Specialist Wellness Counsellor, SWC25/9820

How I can help

Sessions are held in person at the practice in Langeberg Ridge. You do not have to carry this alone, and things can get better.

Counselling for individuals

Emotional wellbeing, trauma, mental wellness, personal growth, relationships and behaviour. A safe and compassionate space to explore life's challenges, process difficult experiences, gain insight and work towards greater emotional wellbeing.

Counselling for couples

Helping couples develop healthier communication, gain insight into relationship patterns, navigate challenges and build a stronger connection.

R750 per session

Children and teenagers

Age-appropriate counselling using play intervention and art techniques, so younger clients can express what they may not yet have words for.

R450 per session

Pregnancy and postpartum

Gentle support through the changes, worries and losses that can come with pregnancy, birth and the months after.

R450 per session
